# Gabriel S. Ayayia

**PhD researcher, Biological & Ecological Engineering — Oregon State University**  
**Founder & Executive Director — LIFEWS (Nigeria)**

I research agrivoltaics, irrigation–energy coupling, soil–water processes, and integrated food–energy–water (FEW) decision systems. I initiate and lead research-stage computational work to investigate the interaction of agricultural production, irrigation, solar generation, energy storage, operational decisions, and resilience.

## Research software and development evidence

### [CHIPU-FEW Intelligence](https://github.com/Profgabby/chipu-few-intelligence)
**Research lead:** Gabriel S. Ayayia. I defined the research questions, architecture, modeling requirements, scenario comparisons, and interpretation of the prototype's outputs. The project evolved from CropSmart Research Digital Twin and retains that history. It is an experimental decision-support framework, **not a field-validated farm-control product**.
