## Scientific scope

This is an annual manual-input engineering screen. It does not consume the globally selected site's weather, HYDRUS outputs, or observed datasets. The context boundary permits this independent workspace without substituting Cedar Creek data. Warnings explicitly distinguish the site's header from the laboratory's inputs.

Each model has editable PV density, performance ratio, annual plane-of-array irradiation, irrigation demand ratio, application efficiency, capital cost and annual operating cost. Default densities differ solely to demonstrate propagation of illustrative configurations; they are not published specifications, validated geometry, or inferred from imagery. Defaults share irradiation, performance ratio, costs and irrigation parameters; default water savings are zero. No advantage for tracking, shade, crop response or scale is asserted.

Equal-land comparison fixes hectares. Equal-capacity comparison fixes installed kW and derives required hectares from each density. Each baseline uses the same model-specific land area, common net irrigation depth and common baseline efficiency. Per-hectare irrigation and PV outputs support interpretation when areas differ. Negative water savings are retained; zero baseline produces null percentage.

PV uses annual plane-of-array irradiation, not horizontal irradiation. Irrigation depth conversion is 10 m³ per ha per mm. Pumping uses water density 1000 kg/m³, g=9.81 m/s², total dynamic head and wire-to-water efficiency. Pump supply timing, batteries, self-consumption and grid imports are not modeled.

Economic outputs use constant annual incremental electricity cash flow against open sun: PV generation plus avoided pumping, valued at one user-entered blended electricity price, minus annual OPEX. NPV and annualization use the entered lifetime and discount rate; zero discount is handled explicitly. Payback is undiscounted and may exceed lifetime. LCOE is a simplified PV-system cost metric. All money is USD. Crop income, water tariffs, depreciation, degradation, tax, replacements and financing are excluded.

## Reproducibility and validation

JSON export carries schema version, timestamp, selected IDs, full per-model inputs, shared settings, results, evidence level and limitations. Changes invalidate displayed results and disable export until recalculation. Values are session-only and are not persisted automatically.

`node --test tests/*.test.mjs` verifies image coverage, water/energy units, baseline comparisons, equal-capacity and equal-land behavior, economic edge cases, invalid inputs, and existing tests. CI runs the complete suite and production build.
