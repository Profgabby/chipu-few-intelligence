## Why legacy identifiers remain

Renaming every internal identifier would break or obscure parts of the reproducibility record. Legacy names remain where they identify:
- IndexedDB stores;
- PostgreSQL tables/migrations;
- environment/configuration contracts;
- historical release documents;
- old routes maintained for compatibility; or
- historical commit/release context.

They should be interpreted as technical lineage, not as competing current product identities.
