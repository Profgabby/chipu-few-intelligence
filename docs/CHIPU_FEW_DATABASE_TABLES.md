# New operational tables

`cfew_places` stores site context. `cfew_stakeholders` and `cfew_stakeholder_preferences` store documented human decision context. `cfew_resilience_scenarios` and `cfew_resilience_results` store scenario definitions and derived assessments. `cfew_economic_cases` and `cfew_economic_results` store documented TEA assumptions and calculated outputs. All are additive to the existing `cs_*` research schema.
